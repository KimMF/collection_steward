# Intake is independent of checkout

Incoming donations arrive at unpredictable times and are recorded through an
intake workflow. Intake does not check an asset out to a production, even when
an intended future use is already known.

The initial intake screen is designed for a nontechnical user who is not a
costumer. Only a short item name is required. Photographs and descriptive
information are recorded when known, and uncertain information may be left for
later costumer review.
