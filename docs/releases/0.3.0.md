# Collection Steward 0.3.0 — Review, Productions, Measurements, and Fittings

Version 3 turns the Version 2 pilot into a practical stewardship workspace for
reviewing collection assets, managing productions and casts, planning grouped
measurement sessions, preserving measurement changes, and recording simple
fittings.

The release deliberately stops short of a full costume-plot or work-order
system. Existing permanent IDs and historical records remain central: routine
workflows retire, deactivate, cancel, complete, or append history rather than
deleting established records.

## Asset stewardship

- Every new intake asset enters an **Assets awaiting review** queue.
- Administrators and stewards can queue an existing asset for review.
- The review form corrects structured descriptions, acquisition information,
  location, notes, tags, and the primary photograph.
- Dated condition reviews record smell, stains, damage, wear, and general
  condition as Clear, Issue found, or Not assessed.
- Asset names use the approved structured format when deliberately reviewed.
- Assets can be retired with a disposition, date, note, responsible user, and
  permanent lifecycle history.
- Retired assets are excluded from ordinary browsing and checkout while an
  authorized **Include retired assets** control makes them available for
  historical review.

## Productions and cast

- A dedicated **Productions** page creates and maintains productions, venues,
  dates, years, status, and cast assignments.
- One person may portray more than one character in a production.
- Cast assignments can be deactivated without removing linked measurement,
  fitting, or checkout history.
- Completed and cancelled productions remain accessible as history.

## Production measurement sessions

- A production can have multiple grouped measurement sessions.
- A session selects its participating cast, date, reusable template, and
  shared measurement fields.
- Additional actor-specific fields do not alter the shared template.
- Participants can be added after the session is created.
- Printable blank worksheets retain complete spreadsheet cell borders.
- Completing or reopening a production session preserves its actor records.
- Existing individual, unscheduled measurement sessions remain supported.

## Immutable measurement history

- Every recorded, accepted, corrected, removed, or Not applicable measurement
  change receives a separate append-only history event.
- History records the prior and new values, action, reason or source, user, and
  timestamp.
- Migration 022 establishes baseline events for values that existed when
  tracking began without changing imported source or staging data.
- Measurement history can be viewed in compact or expanded form and cannot be
  edited or deleted through the application.

## Simple fittings

- A fitting belongs to one production actor/character assignment and holds
  candidate collection assets for that person to try.
- Pull lists display permanent asset identity, photograph, type, size, color,
  location, and availability and are printable.
- Results distinguish **Selected for wear** from **Not selected during
  fitting** while retaining both as fitting history.
- Selected assets use the established production-checkout workflow; correcting
  a test selection cancels its linked checkout without deleting history.
- Physical fitting-tag text and controlled result tags are preserved.
- Initial work tags include Needs alteration, Needs laundering, and Needs
  repair; additional controlled tags remain possible.
- Completed fittings can be reopened for corrections without losing their
  candidates, notes, tags, measurement link, or checkout links.
- Separate print controls provide the full fitting record and a list containing
  only assets selected for wear.

## Administrator data maintenance

- Administrators receive a restricted **Data maintenance** workspace that is
  not available to stewards or intake-only users.
- Fixed integrity checks cover review queues, vocabulary suggestions, flagged
  measurements, pending fitting results, and asset/checkout mismatches.
- Allowlisted searches cover assets, people, productions, measurements,
  checkouts, fittings, users, vocabulary, and administrator history.
- Existing records link to their normal validated Collection Steward editor.
- Missing correction paths for person identities and controlled asset
  vocabulary receive dedicated validated forms with required reasons.
- Each direct maintenance correction creates an append-only before/after audit
  event. The page exposes neither arbitrary SQL nor record deletion.

## Access and compatibility

- Administrator, Steward, and Intake-only permissions remain distinct.
- Administrators alone manage user accounts and administrator data
  maintenance.
- Administrators and stewards share asset review, production, measurements,
  fitting, checkout, and vocabulary-review work.
- Intake-only accounts remain limited to intake.
- The Version 2 intake, browsing, vocabulary review, measurements, printing,
  checkout, user administration, password-change, compact-view, and phone
  behavior are retained.

## Database migrations

When upgrading from the tagged `v0.2.2` release, back up the database and run
these one-time migrations in order before deploying the matching PHP files:

1. `018_add_asset_review.sql`
2. `019_add_asset_retirement.sql`
3. `020_add_production_management.sql`
4. `021_add_production_measurement_sessions.sql`
5. `022_add_measurement_value_history.sql`
6. `023_add_simple_fittings.sql`
7. `024_add_administrative_change_history.sql`

Each checkpoint's documentation records its deployment, verification, and
rollback procedure. Do not rerun a migration that has already completed.

## Deliberate Version 3 limits

- No scene-by-scene or look-by-look costume plot
- No full alteration, repair, laundering, or other work-management workflow
- No automatic fit recommendations
- No scalable asset-search selector for production checkout; planned for
  Version 4
- No administrator password recovery codes or generated-password tool; planned
  for Version 4
- Deployment remains a deliberate manual backup, migration, file replacement,
  live test, and Git synchronization process
